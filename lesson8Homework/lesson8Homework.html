Arcane

Welcome to the World of Arcane

Step into the vivid universe of Arcane, the groundbreaking animated series inspired by League of Legends. More than just a show, Arcane is a cinematic masterpiece that blends art, emotion, and storytelling into a breathtaking exploration of power, identity, and destiny.

Piltover

Two Cities, One Conflict

Set between the utopian city of Piltover and the oppressed undercity of Zaun, Arcane tells the story of two sisters — Vi and Jinx — whose bond is torn apart by ambition, invention, and revolution. The series immerses viewers in a world where technology and magic collide, revealing how progress can both build and destroy.

A Story Beyond the Game

Even if you’ve never played League of Legends, Arcane stands on its own as a deep, character-driven narrative. Every frame is hand-crafted with emotion and detail, capturing the essence of human struggle — love, loss, and the search for purpose — in a world teetering on chaos.

Artistry and Innovation
