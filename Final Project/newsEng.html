Important Tips for Travelers

Tip: Travel to Europe:

If you are planning an international tour, please check the expiration date of your passport. To enter European countries, it must be valid for at least 6 months longer than your planned return date.

Tip: Local Tours:

For visiting some sights in Kakheti and Samtskhe-Javakheti (Vardzia), warm and waterproof footwear is recommended, especially from November to March.
