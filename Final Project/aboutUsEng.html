About Us

Welcome to Turoperatori.ge – your trusted guide to a world of unforgettable journeys! We are a tourism company specializing in discovering the unique corners of Georgia as well as creating unforgettable adventures in the world's most captivating cities. Our goal is for every tour to be not just a trip, but a valuable, cultural, and emotional experience.

Our Mission and Vision

Our mission is to offer the highest quality, customized, and accessible travel services.

  • Local Expertise: We will introduce you to the true soul of Georgia – ancient cultural monuments (Mtskheta, Uplistsikhe, Vardzia), beautiful nature (Mestia, Borjomi), and famous wine regions (Kakheti).
  • Global Opportunities: Our international tours cover major European cities (Paris, Rome, London), allowing you to travel comfortably around the world.
  • Individual Approach: We believe that every journey is unique. Our team is ready to create itineraries tailored to you, taking into account your interests and budget.

Why Choose Us?

Our advantage lies in our experience and dedication to quality:

  • Full Service: We take care of everything, from planning tours and guide services to partnering with reliable airline companies.
  • Professional Guides: Our local guides are not only knowledgeable about history and culture but are also excellent storytellers who will make your journey special.
  • Transparent Pricing: We offer competitive prices without any hidden fees, allowing you to maximize the enjoyment of your trip.

Our Team

Turoperatori.ge was founded by Aleksander Pataraia in 2025. Our team consists of travel enthusiasts and qualified professionals who work every day to turn your travel dreams into reality.

Contact us today and plan your next unforgettable adventure!
